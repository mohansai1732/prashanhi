Treatments

Comprehensive Dermatological Care

Clinical and cosmetic treatments tailored to individual skin types, conditions, and goals.

Acne & Acne Scars

Medical management of active acne and advanced scar revision techniques including peels and microneedling.

Pigmentation & Melasma

Targeted treatment for dark spots, uneven skin tone, melasma, and sun damage with clinical-grade protocols.

Hair & Scalp Care

Diagnosis and treatment of hair loss, dandruff, alopecia, and scalp conditions. PRP therapy available.

Skin Allergies & Eczema

Evaluation and management of allergic dermatitis, eczema, urticaria, and chronic skin sensitivities.

Cosmetic Dermatology

Anti-aging treatments, chemical peels, skin rejuvenation, and aesthetic procedures for healthy, glowing skin.

Laser Treatments

Advanced laser therapy for hair removal, scar reduction, skin resurfacing, and pigmentation correction.
